About Swaram

Our Mission

Swaram exists to make music accessible to everyone. Whether you're a beginner picking up your first guitar or an experienced musician learning a new song, finding accurate chords shouldn't be difficult or expensive.

We built Swaram as a completely free platform — no subscriptions, no paywalls, no limits. Upload any song, in any language, from any genre, and our AI will detect the chords instantly. That's it. Music belongs to everyone, and the tools to learn it should too.

What We Offer

AI Chord Finder — Our flagship tool. Upload any audio file and get accurate chord detection with precise timing. Works with any genre, any language, any instrument. Powered by advanced signal processing and machine learning algorithms that analyze harmonic content in real-time.

Chord Identifier — Enter the notes you're playing and instantly identify the chord name. Perfect for when you discover a shape on guitar or piano and want to know what you're actually playing.

Chord Progressions — Explore common chord progressions in all 24 major and minor keys. Essential for songwriters, improvisers, and anyone learning music theory.

Song Library — A growing collection of over 1,000 songs with AI-detected chords, plus hand-curated chord charts for Malayalam Christian devotional music with precise timing and structure.

Learning Resources — Free guides on reading chord charts, transposing, common progressions, beginner chords, and how AI chord detection works.

How Our AI Works

Swaram's chord detection engine uses a multi-stage signal processing pipeline:

  1. Audio Analysis — We convert audio to a spectral representation using Constant-Q Transform (CQT), which maps frequencies to musical pitches with high precision.
  2. Chroma Feature Extraction — The CQT spectrum is folded into 12 chroma bins (one per semitone), creating a "fingerprint" of which notes are present at each moment.
  3. Chord Recognition — Chroma vectors are matched against chord templates using correlation-based matching, with key context used to resolve ambiguities.
  4. Post-Processing — Results are refined using musical knowledge: key estimation, chord smoothing, beat alignment, and complexity penalties to prefer simpler (more likely) interpretations.

The entire system runs on our backend service, processes audio in seconds, and returns chords with beat-level timing accuracy.

Our Story

Swaram started in 2024 as a personal project to solve a specific problem: finding guitar chords for Malayalam Christian devotional songs. These songs are rarely covered by mainstream chord sites, leaving church musicians to figure things out by ear.

As the AI chord detection technology improved, we realized it could help musicians everywhere — not just in one language or genre. Today, Swaram serves musicians worldwide, detecting chords for everything from Bollywood hits to K-pop, classical to metal, folk songs to film soundtracks.

The name "Swaram" comes from the Sanskrit/Malayalam word for "musical note" (സ്വരം) — the fundamental building block of all music.

Built With

  • Frontend — Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no bloat. Fast on any device.
  • AI Backend — Python with librosa for audio analysis, FastAPI for the service layer.
  • Hosting — Static site on Netlify with global CDN for fast loading worldwide.
  • Open Philosophy — We believe in making music tools accessible. The chord finder is free with no usage limits.
