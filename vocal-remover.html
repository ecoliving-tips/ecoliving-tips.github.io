Frequently Asked Questions

How does the stem separator work?

Your audio is sent to our server running Demucs htdemucs — Meta’s state-of-the-art hybrid transformer model. It separates the mix into 4 independent stems (vocals, drums, bass, other) which are returned as downloadable MP3 files.

How long does it take?

Typically 5–10 minutes for a 3–4 minute song on our CPU server. The quality is significantly better than instant browser-based tools. A progress bar shows live status while you wait.

Can I isolate just the drums or bass?

Yes — Demucs separates all 4 stems at once (vocals, drums, bass, other instruments), so you can download whichever stem you need. You also get a pre-mixed karaoke track (drums + bass + other combined).

What audio formats are supported?

MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC, AAC, and WebM files up to 30 MB. Maximum audio length is 5 minutes. All stems are returned as MP3 files (192 kbps).

Can I use the stems for covers or remixes?

Stems can be used for personal practice, cover recordings, and remixes. Ensure you have appropriate rights before using in any public or commercial release.
