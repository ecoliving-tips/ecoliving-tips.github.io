Frequently Asked Questions

How does the chord identifier work?

Play a chord on your instrument near your device's microphone. The tool listens in real-time, detects pitch frequencies, identifies the notes, and matches them to known chord patterns to show you the chord name and diagrams.

What instruments does it work with?

Any pitched instrument — guitar, piano, ukulele, keyboard, mandolin, banjo, and more. Works best with a single instrument playing a clear chord in a quiet environment.

Does it record my audio?

No. All audio processing happens locally in your browser using the Web Audio API. No audio data is sent to any server. Your privacy is fully protected.

How many chords can it recognize?

The chord identifier recognizes 221 chords across all 12 keys, including major, minor, 7th, major 7th, minor 7th, sus2, sus4, diminished, augmented, 6th, 9th, and half-diminished chords.
